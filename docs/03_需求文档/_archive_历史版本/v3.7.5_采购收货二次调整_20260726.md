## 关键改动

### 后端 (Java)
- `PurchaseOrderController.java`
  - `submit()/approve()` @OperationLog remark 从 "PO-submit/approve" 改为中文
  - `cancel()` 守卫: 仅 DRAFT / APPROVED 允许取消 (剔除 SUBMITTED)
  - `allowedActions(SUBMITTED)` 移除 cancel action
  - list/detail/export SQL: `COALESCE(NULLIF(po.supplier_name,''), s.name)`, JOIN suppliers
- `ReceiptBatchService.java`
  - 19 处 RuntimeException → BusinessException(BUSINESS_RULE_VIOLATION)
  - 6 条友好中文错误 (over-PO qty/batch_no/qty/serial required/serial mismatch/lines empty)
- `BizDocDetailController.java`
  - `poDetail()` 追加 `allowedActions` (为一致性;前端目前不依赖)

### 前端 (Vue)
- `modules.js` (purchase-orders)
  - `noDelete: true`, `editableWhen: ['DRAFT']`
  - 取消 action `when: ['DRAFT', 'APPROVED']`
- `CrudView.vue`
  - 新增 `rowEditable(row)/rowDeletable(row)`, 支持 `editableWhen/deletableWhen` row-level 控制
  - 新增 `dictLabel(col, v)`: 有 `filter.options` 的列自动做 label 翻译
- `ReceiptEdit.vue`
  - PO 采购产品明细卡片 (v3.7.4 已加); 待收/已收/采购数量列
  - v-if 判断 poLines 存在
